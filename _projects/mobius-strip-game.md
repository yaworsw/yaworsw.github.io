An action racing game built for the [Cursor Vibejam](https://vibej.am/2026/) in about two weeks. Up to 8 players race together online, taking cues from the arcade-y handling of [Mario Kart 64](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mario_Kart_64), the high-speed feel of [F-Zero X](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/F-Zero_X), and the combat of [Road Rash II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Road_Rash_II).
